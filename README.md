---

### 🔍 About My Research
My work focuses on **AI-driven behavioral biomarkers** for healthcare applications — particularly the **early detection of Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI)** and **fall-risk prediction** using **wearable sensors**, **gait/balance analysis**, and **speech processing**.  
I’m passionate about bridging **academic research and practical AI applications** in digital health, rehabilitation, and preventive diagnostics.

---

### 🧪 Selected Research Contributions
- **Falls-Risk Dataset (GIST, 2017):** Publicly available dataset for human balance and fall-risk analysis using inertial sensors.  
- **Gait & Balance Analytics for MCI Detection (2022–2025):** Developed machine learning and deep learning frameworks for early cognitive health screening.  
- **Multimodal AI Models (2025–present):** Integrating gait, balance, and speech modalities for non-invasive dementia detection.  

---
